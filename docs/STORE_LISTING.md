## Data safety (Play) — suggested answers (owner must confirm)
- Collected: email, name, phone (account); precise location of the user's own device (only in the foreground, to show the user on the map); location of the tracked vehicles (device data the customer owns). 
- Shared with third parties: no. Sold: no. Encrypted in transit: yes (HTTPS). Deletion request: yes, `https://athargps.com/account-deletion`.
- No ads, no analytics SDKs in the app.
